What we evaluate

What we evaluate before
you make a decision

Modelled on the occupational psychology framework used by senior hiring managers when screening candidates for competitive roles.

🎯
Role Clarity & Seniority
Can we clearly identify what this candidate does — and at what level? Vague positioning is one of the most common reasons a strong candidate is wrongly passed over or wrongly advanced.
📈
Evidence of Impact
Are there measurable results, or just responsibilities? Candidates who demonstrate quantified outcomes are significantly more likely to perform in role than those who only list duties.
🔗
Alignment to Role Requirements
Does this candidate actually match what you're hiring for? Experience that looks relevant on the surface often conceals critical gaps at the functional or seniority level.
📅
Career Consistency
Are there gaps, contradictions, or unexplained transitions? Inconsistencies in career history are among the strongest early predictors of future retention risk.
⚠️
Behavioural Risk Indicators
Patterns that may signal future workplace conflict, disengagement, or resistance — identified before the interview stage, not discovered after the hire.
